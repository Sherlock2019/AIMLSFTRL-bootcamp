# Delivery policy

Never promise a delivery date before checking warehouse stock and delivery slot availability in the system.
Standard home delivery takes 2 to 5 working days. Next-day delivery is available for orders placed before 2pm
when the item is in warehouse stock and a next-day slot is free.

Large items (sheds, tool chests, appliances) need a two-person delivery slot, which must be booked separately.
Same-day delivery is not guaranteed and is only offered in the metro zone for small items.

Building-site deliveries need a site contact name and a safe unloading area. Bulk orders over 1 tonne
(for example 50 bags of cement) are delivered by the builders' merchant truck on fixed weekdays.
