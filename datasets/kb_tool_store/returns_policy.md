# Returns policy

Customers can return most items within 30 days of purchase with a receipt or proof of purchase.
Refunds always go back to the original payment method. Cash refunds are only given for cash purchases.

Without a receipt, look up the purchase using the customer's card or loyalty account. If no proof of purchase
is found, the customer can receive store credit at the lowest selling price from the last 90 days.

Opened hardware (screws, fixings, small parts) can be returned if it is unused and within 30 days.
Power tools that have been used can only be returned if they are faulty; otherwise use the warranty process.
Custom-cut items (timber, chain, carpet, mixed paint) cannot be returned unless they are faulty.
