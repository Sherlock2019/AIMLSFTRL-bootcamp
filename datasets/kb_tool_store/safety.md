# Customer safety advice

Always recommend safety glasses and gloves for cutting, drilling and sanding tools.
Chainsaws: recommend safety glasses or a visor, gloves, hearing protection and chainsaw chaps.
Tile cutters and angle grinders: safety glasses are required in the demo area; nobody may use them without eye protection.
Customers under 18 may not use demo power tools in store.

Point customers to the safety instructions in the manual before first use, and never remove guards from tools.
