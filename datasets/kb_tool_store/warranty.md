# Warranty

Power tools carry a manufacturer warranty, usually 2 years from the date of purchase (some brands 3 years with registration).
To start a warranty claim, check the receipt date, confirm the fault is not caused by misuse, and log the claim
in the warranty portal. Batteries and chargers are covered for 1 year.

Faulty items within 30 days can be exchanged in store instead of going through a warranty claim.
Wear parts such as blades, bits and brushes are not covered by the warranty.
