# Discounts and price match

Staff cannot give discounts on their own. Any manual discount needs a manager's approval in the till system.

Price match: we match a local competitor's current advertised price for the identical item (same brand and model
number) when it is in stock at the competitor. Verify the price on the competitor's website or flyer before agreeing.
Price match does not apply to clearance items, marketplace sellers or membership-only prices.

Discount codes must be checked for expiry date and conditions. If a valid code fails at the till, a manager can apply it manually.
